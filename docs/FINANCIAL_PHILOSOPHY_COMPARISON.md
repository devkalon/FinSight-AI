## Supported Dimensions
The engine analyzes scenarios across seven financial dimensions:
1. **Budgeting**: 50/30/20 allocation vs Conscious Spending Plan vs Zero-based cash flows.
2. **Saving**: Liquid emergency buffers vs aggressive deployment vs sub-savings accounts.
3. **Spending**: High intentionality / Money Dials vs frugality vs asset-funded luxury.
4. **Debt**: Elimination of bad consumer debt vs strategic low-cost leverage for cash-flowing assets.
5. **Investing**: Low-cost broad market index compounding vs direct real estate/business cashflow.
6. **Financial Goals**: Retirement compounding vs cashflow independence vs family/tax milestones.
7. **Lifestyle Spending**: Guilt-free indulgence on core priorities vs living below means.
